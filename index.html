Sonko — Ugandan Courtyard Hopscotch

Play Sonko in 3D: toss your Ejjinja river stone, hop across sunlit murram chalk squares on one foot, steer through courtyard breezes and puddles, and claim Houses across five authentic East African worlds.

Game Highlights & Modes

Challenge AI rivals, play local 2-Player pass-and-play or 2v2 Teams, or join live Online 1v1 rooms. Explore the Primary Schoolyard, Kampala Compound, Entebbe Lakeshore, Jinja Rain Garden, and Ndere Night Pavilion with dynamic weather, synced wind-swaying trees, Daily Missions, and custom Courtyard Stall outfits.

How to Play

Hold Click or Space to toss your stone into the target square, tap or press Space to hop while avoiding lines and opponent Houses, balance with A/D keys, scoop your stone on the return trip, and perform a blind over-the-shoulder toss to claim your own House.
